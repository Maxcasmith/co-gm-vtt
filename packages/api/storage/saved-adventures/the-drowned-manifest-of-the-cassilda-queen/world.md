# The Drowned Manifest of the *Cassilda Queen*

Three weeks ago the luxury liner *Cassilda Queen*, missing since she vanished off the New England coast in 1929, drifted out of a fog bank and grounded herself on the rocks beyond Innsmouth — her hull barnacled with things that have no business growing in Atlantic water. The party has been quietly engaged to board her before the authorities or the Esoteric Order of Dagon can reach her first, charged with recovering the ship's manifest and a locked strongbox from the purser's office that a certain anxious scholar believes contains proof of what the liner's final voyage was really carrying. Survivors' families speak in whispers of a detour, a nameless reef on no chart, and passengers who came back aboard changed. The ship groans against the rocks as if breathing, saltwater weeping from her portholes, and the tide is rising — whoever goes in must be out before she floats free and slides back into the deep.

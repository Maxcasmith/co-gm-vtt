# Detention of the Damned

It's the last day of term at Brightwater Academy of Arcane Arts, and the party — a ragtag study group of senior students one exam short of graduating — has been handed an ultimatum by the proctors: descend into the old spellwork vaults beneath the gymnasium and retrieve the founding headmaster's long-lost Grimoire of Unfailing Answers, or repeat the entire year. Rumor among the upperclassmen says the vaults were sealed after a summoning class went hilariously wrong decades ago, and that the wards now behave like an overzealous hall monitor with a taste for trick questions. Armed with borrowed wands, a cafeteria tray repurposed as a shield, and a hall pass signed in shimmering ink, the would-be graduates clatter down the steps beneath the trophy case, laughing a little too loudly. The mood is mischievous and light — less grim delving, more magical hijinks with extra credit on the line. Somewhere below, something is grading their approach.

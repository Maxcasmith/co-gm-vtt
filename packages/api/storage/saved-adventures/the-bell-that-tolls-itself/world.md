# The Bell That Tolls Itself

Three nights ago, the chapel of Saint Merrow began ringing its own bell at midnight — and the gravedigger who went to investigate came back wrong, grey-faced and whispering scripture backwards. The village's remaining faithful have pooled their funeral silver and begged the adventurers to enter the church and stop whatever has been clawing its way up from the crypt beneath the altar, because the dead in the churchyard are no longer staying buried. Something older than the congregation has awakened in the consecrated ground, twisting holiness into hunger, and every toll of the bell calls another soul to its feet. The party steps through doors that no longer close behind the living, carrying torches and prayers in equal measure, knowing that what waits inside wears the robes of priests and the faces of the recently mourned.
